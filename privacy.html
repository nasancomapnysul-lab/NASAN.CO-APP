Privacy Policy

nasan Company · Sulaymaniyah, Iraq · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what the nasan Company app collects, why, and how you can delete it. The app is for adults (18 and over).

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored scrambled, never in plain text, and are checked by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Sign in with Google: if you use it, Google gives us your name, email address and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile photo: a photo of you, asked for when you create an account, so our team knows who picks up the order. You can change it any time.
  • Address and shop details: your city, street address and, if you have a shop, its name and address.
  • Location: only if you allow it, your precise location is saved on your account so we can find your shop or address.
  • Orders: the products you order, the quantity, the date and the order status.
  • Reviews: if you rate a product, your stars, comment and first name with last initial (for example "Karwan H.") are shown on that product after we check them.
  • Camera: only when you tap Scan a code, to read the product code on a box. Pictures are not saved or sent to us.
  • Photos you choose: if you send a photo of your screen to ask which LCD fits, it goes through your phone's share menu (for example WhatsApp) straight to us. The app does not keep it.
  • Notifications: only if you allow them, to tell you when your order is ready or a product is back in stock.
  • Saved on your phone only: your favourites, recently viewed products, products you compare and your light/dark setting. These stay on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • App usage: which screens are opened, what is searched, what is added to the cart, device type, browser, language, a random device code, loading speed and app errors, to fix problems and improve the app. If you are signed in, items added to your cart may be linked to your account so we can help you with a price.
  • Security checks: Google reCAPTCHA (part of Firebase App Check) checks that requests come from the real app. Google may collect device and browser information for this.

Why we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email address and let you sign in.
  • To prepare your orders for pickup and follow up on them.
  • To contact you about your orders by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To show customer levels (Member, Silver, Gold) based on orders you picked up.
  • To protect the app and our customers from abuse and fake accounts.

We do not sell your data and do not show ads. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC) and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Account emails, such as email confirmation and password reset, are sent by Google Firebase.

Who can see it

Only nasan Company staff can see customer details and orders. Approved reviews show your first name and last initial to other app users. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Delete your account

To delete your account and personal data, or only some of it, send us a message from the phone number or email on your account:

  • WhatsApp: +964 770 414 9292
  • Email: info@nasan.company

Within 7 days we delete your account, sign-in, address, shop details, location, photo and reviews. Order records may be kept up to 1 year for accounting, with your name removed. If an account was blocked for abuse, we keep only a scrambled code made from its email and phone number, so it cannot be used to make a new account.

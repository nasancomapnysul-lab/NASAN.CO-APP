Privacy Policy

nasan Company · Sulaymaniyah, Iraq · Last updated 11 November 2026

This policy explains what the nasan Company app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, phone number and password (stored scrambled, never in plain text).
  • Address and shop details: your city, street address and, if you have a shop, its name and address.
  • Location: only if you allow it, to fill in your delivery address.
  • Profile photo: only if you add one.
  • Orders: the products you order and their status.
  • Reviews: if you rate a product, your stars, comment and first name with last initial (for example "Karwan H.") are shown on that product after we check them.
  • Camera: only when you tap Scan a code, to read the product code on a box. Pictures are not saved or sent to us.
  • Photos you choose: if you send a photo of your screen to ask which LCD fits, it goes through your phone's share menu (for example WhatsApp) straight to us. The app does not keep it.
  • Saved on your phone only: your favourites, recently viewed products, products you compare and your light/dark setting. These stay on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • App usage: which screens are opened, what is searched, what is added to the cart, device type and language, to improve the app. If you are signed in, items added to your cart may be linked to your account so we can help you with a price.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To prepare, deliver and follow up on your orders.
  • To contact you about your orders by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To show customer levels (Member, Silver, Gold) based on orders you picked up.

We do not sell your data and do not show ads. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC) and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Who can see it

Only nasan Company staff with an admin account can see customer details and orders. Approved reviews show your first name and last initial to other app users. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Delete your account

To delete your account and personal data, send us a message from the phone number or email on your account:

  • WhatsApp: +964 770 414 9292
  • Email: info@nasan.company

We delete your account, address, shop details, location and photo within 7 days. Order records may be kept up to 1 year for accounting, with your name removed.

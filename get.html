nasan Company

Repair tools, microscopes, power supplies and LCDs. Sulaymaniyah, Iraq.

Android Your phone

Download the app and install it. About 2 MB.

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file nasan.apk and tap Install.
  3. If it says Install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  4. Open nasan Company from your home screen.

iPhone & iPad Your phone

No download needed. Add the app to your home screen from Safari.

Open the app in Safari
  1. Open nasancomapnysul-lab.github.io/NASAN.CO-APP in Safari (not Chrome or WhatsApp).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open nasan from your home screen. It opens full screen.
Use the app in the browser

The app updates by itself. You only install it once.
